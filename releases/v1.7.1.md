## Verification and limits

711 deterministic DSP/geometry checks, offline native spatial rendering and muted local-speech integration tests pass. Both executable architectures and the packaged app signature are checked. These checks do not establish human intelligibility, sound-pressure calibration or physical sound placement. The app was exercised on macOS 26.3 Apple silicon; macOS 14 and Intel runtime checks were not performed. Full Xcode IDE builds were not run; SwiftPM and direct Swift compiler builds were verified.

The room is simulated, not lidar data. Carrier-only output cannot reproduce intelligible words. Clear words uses ordinary audible speech bandwidth. HRTF is a headphone spatial cue model. No microphone capture, camera/lidar capture, head tracking, networking, haptics or background monitoring is implemented.
